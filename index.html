Executive Office Address • Formal Message from the Chief Executive Officer

"The global transition toward electrification and industrial high-technology has placed unprecedented demands on the world’s critical mineral supply chain. In this global re-alignment, Pakistan stands on the precipice of a historic emergence."

Our northern terrains—where the Karakoram, Tethyan, and Hindu Kush tectonic plates converge—hold some of the planet’s least exploited and most highly prospective polymetallic and rare element systems. Historically, access to these assets was constrained by topography, insufficient technical data, and fragmented regulatory oversight. Real and Rare (Pvt.) Ltd. was founded specifically to eliminate those bottlenecks.

We provide sovereign-grade regulatory certainty, meticulous local operational infrastructure, and a modern commitment to ESG principles that prioritize the mountain communities of Gilgit-Baltistan. We extend an open, institutional invitation to global mining conglomerates, institutional exploration funds, and off-take strategists: join us in unlocking Pakistan’s vast natural heritage under clear, mutually rewarding joint venture frameworks built for generational success.
